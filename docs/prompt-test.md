# 3 AI Solutions for a Baker Who Is Not Getting Orders

**Problem:** A baker is struggling to get customer orders and needs effective ways to promote products, attract customers, and increase sales.

## Solution 1: AI-Powered Social Media Marketing

**Description:** Use AI tools such as ChatGPT and Canva to create attractive promotional posts, product descriptions, captions, and marketing content for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.

**How it Helps:** Regular and engaging content can increase brand awareness, reach potential customers, and encourage more people to place orders.

## Solution 2: AI Customer Support Chatbot

**Description:** Use an AI chatbot to answer customer questions about cake flavours, prices, customization options, delivery details, and order procedures.

**How it Helps:** Quick responses improve customer service, save the baker's time, and make it easier for potential customers to place orders.

## Solution 3: AI-Based Promotions and Offers

**Description:** Use AI tools to suggest promotional strategies, identify popular bakery products, and create special offers for birthdays, weddings, Eid, and other occasions.

**How it Helps:** Targeted promotions can attract new customers, encourage repeat purchases, and increase the chances of receiving more orders.
